# Security Policy

## Supported Versions

Use the latest released version. Security vulnerabilities will be addressed promptly.

## Reporting Vulnerabilities

Please report security vulnerabilities to security@phlegal-intelligence.ph or open a security-marked issue on GitHub.

## What to Include

- Type of vulnerability
- Affected component(s)
- Steps to reproduce
- Potential impact
- Your contact information

## Response Timeline

- Initial acknowledgment: within 48 hours
- Triage: within 5 business days
- Fix development: case-dependent
- Public disclosure: coordinated with reporter

## Data Privacy

This platform follows Republic Act No. 10173 (Data Privacy Act of 2012) and NPC AI Guidelines. All personal data processing is governed by the privacy specification in `docs/privacy/PRIVACY_SPECIFICATION.md`.
