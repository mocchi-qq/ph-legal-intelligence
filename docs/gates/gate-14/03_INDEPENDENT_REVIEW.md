## Clean areas named by the reviewer

- the activity is fixed by the library and cannot be overridden by a caller;
- the role is sensible for the operation and documented;
- a missing or non-function `authorization` throws before any work;
- the resolver is called once per document rather than once per chunk;
- `IndexAuthorizationRequest` / `IndexAuthorizationDecision` were not exported
  anywhere else, so removing them breaks no consumer;
- the passage contract and `retrieve` are byte-identical;
- the new tests would fail if gating were removed.

## Note on finding 1

The reviewer was right that the original claim was too strong: the _type_ requires
the contract, and TypeScript callers are held to it, but a JavaScript caller or a
cast is not. The remediation validates the answer's **content** — decision literal,
activity, document — which is what makes the claim true at runtime. What it does
not do is reject the resolver itself; a resolver that answers consistently and
correctly for the asked pair is accepted, as it must be, since a resolver is the
decision source by design.
