## What the review changed

Five behaviour changes, all refusals of previously acceptible-but-wrong input, plus
four new tests and one clarifying test. Nothing was relaxed: the module accepts
strictly less than before.

## On finding 4

This is the second time this id scheme has drawn comment — the first was during
authoring, where the opposite order was found to block two claims sharing a passage.
The scheme is now: identity of the claim for assertions, identity of the evidence for
evidence, with the cycle broken toward the claim and the trade-off written in the
source. Two separate reviewers arriving at it from opposite directions and both being
answered by the same test is a reasonable place to leave it.
