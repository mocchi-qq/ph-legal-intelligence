## What must be added before a determination

1. Archived terms of use or copyright notice with retrieval timestamp and URL.
2. Written permission or an identified legal basis reviewed by counsel.
3. Attribution and linking approach accepted by counsel.
4. A signed or verifiable counsel determination recorded against the source and activity.
5. Retention and deletion requirements.
