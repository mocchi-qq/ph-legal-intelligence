# Outstanding Legal Questions

**Prepared:** 2026-11-13  
**Instruction to counsel:** these questions are neutral. They do not assume that any use is permitted.

## A. Ownership and reuse

1. For each of the 25 sources, does the issuing institution own the material, and what copyright or database right applies?
2. Are the legal texts government works, and what does that status permit or prohibit for reproduction, indexing, AI processing and commercial use?
3. Do the website terms of use add restrictions beyond copyright, and do they bind automated access or downstream use?
4. Does a robots.txt directive, a sitemap or public accessibility create any reuse right? (The project assumes it does not.)

## B. Acquisition and storage

5. May each source be acquired automatically, at what rate, and with what restrictions?
6. May original bytes be retained privately for verification, and for how long?
7. May the four owner-authorized pilot documents be retained, or must they be deleted?
8. What deletion, takedown or revocation process is required?

## C. Processing and derived content

9. May text be extracted and indexed for retrieval?
10. May derived embeddings be created and stored?
11. May content be processed by AI systems, including third-party providers, and what data-location or confidentiality limits apply?
12. Is derived text a separate work or a reproduction, and what follows for storage and display?

## D. Display, quotation and distribution

13. Does displaying a short passage with attribution and a link to the source constitute redistribution?
14. What quotation limits, attribution format and context requirements apply?
15. May citation metadata be displayed publicly?
16. May original or derived content be redistributed, and under what licence?

## E. Commercial and retention

17. Is commercial use permitted, and does internal research permission imply commercial permission? (The project assumes it does not.)
18. What retention schedule, access controls and audit records are required?
19. Are there personal-data or sensitive-case restrictions in published decisions that require redaction or exclusion?

## F. Authenticity and dates

20. What evidence establishes approval, publication and effectivity dates for each instrument?
21. What independent second source is acceptable for authenticity corroboration?
22. Can a source's own listing page be used as evidence of its legal dates? (The project assumes it cannot, without more.)
