## Anycase.ai

**Website:** https://anycase.ai
**Pricing (public):** Pro plan ₱999/month, Education plan ₱599/month
**Retrieval date:** 2026-11-09

### Publicly Documented Features
- Daily-updated legal library with 160,000+ laws, cases, and issuances
- Semantic search across Philippine legal corpus
- AI legal analysis with citation support
- Full-text document access
- Document upload and analysis
- AI drafting and document review (DocDraft)
- Free tier, Pro subscription, Education subscription tiers

### Known Limitations
- Philippines-only coverage (no international law)
- Pricing in PHP only, limited international accessibility
- No public API documentation
- Proprietary corpus — bulk access/reuse terms unclear

### Source
Anycase.ai pricing page, legal AI capabilities page, help center (accessed 2026-11-09)

## LexisNexis

**Website:** https://www.lexisnexis.com
**Pricing (public):** Tiered from $114/month (LexisNexis Solo), enterprise quotes
**Retrieval date:** 2026-11-09

### Publicly Documented Features
- 50-state US case law, statutes, regulations
- Lexis+ AI with Protégé personalized AI assistant
- Shepard's Citations (citator)
- Practical Guidance modules
- News and public records
- API available for enterprise customers

### Known Limitations
- Philippines coverage minimal/secondary
- US-centric corpus
- High cost for individual practitioners
- API access restricted to enterprise contracts

### Source
LexisNexis product pages, pricing sheets (accessed 2026-11-09)

## Westlaw

**Website:** https://www.westlaw.com
**Pricing (public):** From $155.35/month for AI-Assisted Research
**Retrieval date:** 2026-11-09

### Publicly Documented Features
- Comprehensive US federal and state case law
- KeyCite citator service
- Westlaw Edge AI-Assisted Research
- Practical Law practice notes
- Statutes, regulations, administrative decisions
- API via Westlaw API (enterprise)

### Known Limitations
- Philippines coverage minimal
- US-centric design
- High per-seat pricing
- No public API for individual developers

### Source
Thomson Reuters Westlaw pricing and features pages (accessed 2026-11-09)

## vLex

**Website:** https://www.vlex.com
**Pricing (public):** No public pricing; quotes reported $69–$399/user/month
**Retrieval date:** 2026-11-09

- 1+ billion legal documents from 100+ countries

## Harvey

**Website:** https://www.harvey.ai
**Pricing (public):** Custom enterprise pricing only; reported 20-seat minimum, 12-month contracts
**Retrieval date:** 2026-11-09

### Publicly Documented Features
- Custom LLM fine-tuned for legal tasks
- Contract analysis, due diligence, research, drafting
- Integration with Microsoft Word
- Enterprise security and compliance (SOC 2, ISO 27001)
- Dedicated customer success

### Known Limitations
- Not built for litigation/trial preparation
- No public pricing or self-serve tier
- High minimum seat count excludes small firms
- Philippines-specific training unclear

### Source
Harvey.ai website, press coverage, legal tech reviews (accessed 2026-11-09)

## ChatGPT (OpenAI)

**Website:** https://chat.openai.com
**Pricing (public):** Plus $23/month, Pro $103/month (approx. PHP conversion)
**Retrieval date:** 2026-11-09

### Publicly Documented Features
- General-purpose reasoning and text generation
- File upload and analysis (PDF, DOCX, images)
- Code interpreter / data analysis
- Custom GPTs
- Web browsing (Plus/Pro)

### Known Limitations
- **17–34% citation hallucination rate** in legal contexts (per multiple independent studies)
- No legal-specific training or grounding
- No citator or authority verification
- No Philippines-specific legal knowledge beyond training cutoff
- Not suitable for professional legal research without verification

### Source
OpenAI pricing page, independent legal AI hallucination studies (Dahl et al., 2024; Mateo et al., 2024) (accessed 2026-11-09)

## Philippine Legal Databases (Official Sources)

### Supreme Court E-Library
**Website:** https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph
**Features:** Supreme Court decisions (1901+), laws (Acts, Commonwealth Acts, Presidential Decrees, Batas Pambansa, Republic Acts), court issuances, executive issuances, treaties
**Limitations:** No AI-assisted search, no citator, no structured case analysis, bulk access terms unclear

### LawPhil Project
**Website:** https://lawphil.net
**Features:** Jurisprudence, statutes, legal materials
**Limitations:** Community-maintained, no official citator, inconsistent coverage, no AI features

### Official Gazette
**Website:** https://www.officialgazette.gov.ph
**Features:** Presidential issuances, official publications
**Limitations:** Executive branch only, no judicial decisions, no AI features

**Retrieval date:** 2026-11-09 (all three)
