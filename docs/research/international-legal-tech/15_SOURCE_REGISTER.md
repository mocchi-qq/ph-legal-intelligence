## 1. Source reliability notes

- **S01–S06, S13** are vendor pages. They are reliable for _what the vendor
  documents_, not for quality, accuracy or performance. Company performance
  figures are recorded as `COMPANY CLAIM`.
- **S07, S08, S10, S11, S14, S15** are official/open primary sources. They are
  reliable for the fields they expose; absence of a feature on the page is not
  proof of absence.
- **S09** is independent and peer-reviewed-style; it is the only source in this
  dossier that tests vendor AI quality.
- **S12** is a secondary compilation with an explicit accuracy disclaimer; it is
  evidence of _terms and caveats_, not of legal accuracy.
- **S16** records access failure. It supports the statement "could not be
  inspected", not any capability claim.
